COMMON QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU
START.

Do I need previous gym experience?

No. The starting point can be adjusted to your experience. Technique and appropriate exercise selection matter more than arriving advanced.

Is this only for fat loss?

No. The framework can support fat loss, muscle gain, recomposition or building a consistent training routine depending on the client.

Is a diet plan included?

Nutrition guidance is part of the coaching direction. The exact level of meal planning should be agreed during enrollment based on the program and your needs.

Will everyone get the same workout?

No. Exercise choices and progression should reflect training history, ability, schedule and goal rather than forcing every client into one template.

What happens after 30 days?

Your progress is reviewed and you can continue into the most suitable Zenith training or coaching option for the next phase.
