OUR STORY

FROM TECHNOLOGY
TO TRANSFORMATION.

Zenith Fitness Hub was opened in 2019 with a simple objective: create a serious training space where people could do more than just show up and use equipment.

Before moving full-time into fitness, founder and coach Anup built his career as a software developer. That background still shapes how Zenith approaches coaching today — with structure, systems, measurement and continuous improvement rather than guesswork.

Over time, fitness moved from a parallel pursuit into the main mission. Zenith now combines a complete training environment with personal coaching, goal-based programming and practical nutrition guidance for members who want measurable progress.
