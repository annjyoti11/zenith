ZENITH FITNESS HUB — V5.5 LEAD & ENQUIRY SYSTEM

What changed
- Every pre-filled Zenith WhatsApp CTA now opens a quick enquiry modal.
- The form captures: name, phone/WhatsApp, primary goal, preferred workout time, and optional notes.
- The clicked service/package is carried into the enquiry automatically.
- Submitting the form opens WhatsApp with a structured, ready-to-send message.
- No backend/database is used; the site does not store form data.
- Call, directions, email, Instagram and Facebook links have lightweight conversion event hooks.
- Added canonical URLs, og:url metadata, sitemap.xml and an updated robots.txt.

Analytics hooks
The JavaScript pushes events to window.dataLayer if/when Google Tag Manager or GA is added later.
Events include:
- enquiry_open
- lead_submit_whatsapp
- click_call
- click_directions
- click_email
- click_instagram
- click_facebook

Important
No Google Analytics tracking ID was invented or embedded. Add your own GA4/GTM ID later if desired.
