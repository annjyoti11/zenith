PERSONAL TRAINING FAQ

BEFORE YOU
START.

Do I need training experience?

No. Personal training can be useful for complete beginners as well as experienced trainees who want more structured programming and feedback.

Will the plan be the same for everyone?

No. The training direction should reflect your goal, schedule, current ability, training history and any practical limitations discussed during assessment.

Is nutrition included?

Practical nutrition guidance can be provided as part of the coaching process. It is not a substitute for medical nutrition therapy where clinical care is required.
